PictaDiary Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the PictaDiary mobile app and related services.

1. Scope

PictaDiary is designed for journaling, note creation, AI-generated image generation, and a personalized experience based on your preferences.

2. Information we process

We may process the following information to provide and operate the app and its services:

  • Account and authentication data: user ID and session data used for sign-in and authentication through Supabase.
  • Usage data: daily usage counters used to manage the image generation limit.
  • App preferences: settings such as language, theme, and image style.
  • User-provided content: text that you submit, such as diary entries or image generation prompts.

3. Confidential and not retained content

  • PictaDiary treats user logs, prompt content, and generated images as confidential.
  • Prompt text is not stored permanently.
  • Generated images are not retained permanently.
  • User content is not used for advertising, profiling, or third-party training purposes.
  • Image generation is performed through FAL.AI with storage disabled for the request content.

4. How information is used

  • Run the app and authenticate users
  • Enforce daily usage limits
  • Remember the settings you choose
  • Deliver the requested AI features
  • Support troubleshooting and service security

5. Third-party services

PictaDiary may rely on third-party services including:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and service infrastructure
  • FAL.AI: AI image generation

These providers may have their own privacy and data processing practices.

6. Data retention

  • Daily usage counters may be kept as long as needed for limit management.
  • App preferences may be stored on the device or within the relevant service.
  • Prompt, log, and generated image content is not stored permanently.
  • Limited records may be retained when required by law or for security purposes.

7. Sharing of information

Personal data may be shared with third-party providers only when necessary to operate the service. User content is not sold or shared with advertisers.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

PictaDiary is not directed to children below the applicable age of digital consent. Children in that age group should not use the app.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. You can contact us to make such a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be published in the app or through the relevant link.

12. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact us through the support channel in the app or the developer contact information provided by the app.
